    Full game rules

    Points per team

    1,000 ÷ your team count. Four is the floor, so 1 to 4 teams is 250 each. 5 teams is 200 each. 10 is 100. 20 is 50, and 20 is the cap.

    Getting in late

    Week 2 gets you 90% of the points. Week 3 gets 80%. Week 4 gets 70%. After Week 4 nobody new gets in.

    The swap

    One swap a season. One team out, one team in, and the new team takes over the old team's points. The fee is 20 × the week, so 20 in Week 1 and 200 in Week 10, straight off your score. No swaps after Week 10.

    What a rank pays

    Your score is what you'd bank if the season ended today. It runs off the AP poll until the playoff committee's first ranking, then the committee's.

    Your max

    The most you can still win. Four ranked teams is 1,000. Ten is 640. Twenty is 340. It drops when a team is out for good.

    Dead weight

    A team that falls out of the Top 25 stays in your bubble and drags it down. It can climb back. A team that is out of the playoff for good hangs under the bubble. It keeps whatever it already earned.

    Season's end

    Each team pays the better of two numbers. What its playoff run paid, which is 20% for making the field, 50% for the quarterfinal and 100% for the Final Four. Or where it finished in the final poll.

    Ties

    First your champ pick. Then how many of your crowned four made the real Final Four. Then the closest call on the championship game score. Still tied, it's split.

    Lock in

    Nothing counts until you lock in. Locking sets your entry week, your crowned four and your final score call. It closes the roster except for your one swap.
