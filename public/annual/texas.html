No. 05 · TexasThe Autopsy

Start with the autopsy

Why 2025 ended · What the offseason answered

Texas went 10–3 in 2025, scoring 35.8 points per game in its 10 wins and 12.7 in its three losses. Heading into the opener, Arch Manning’s second year is the easy story. The 23.1-point split is why Texas rebuilt through the portal and has a top-five ranking to answer for.

The losses came early and late. Texas opened August 30 with a 14–7 loss at Ohio State, scoring one touchdown with a first-year starting quarterback. Florida won 29–21 on October 4. Georgia finished the run on November 16, 35–10. Texas scored 38 points in the three losses, never reaching 22 or its 30.5-point season average.

Photo slotNovember 16 · AthensGeorgia 35, Texas 10
The night the playoff case collapsed. Texas scored 10 points in its third loss. In Athens, the offense again leaned on Manning when the run game did not carry its share.

Georgia made the run-game problem plain. Texas had 1,794 rushing yards in 13 games, 4.2 per carry and five games below 100 rushing yards. Manning had 399 rushing yards and 10 of the team’s 17 rushing touchdowns. He helped Texas escape Kentucky 16–13 and Vanderbilt 34–31, but the quarterback carried too much of the short-yardage load.

Texas still beat Oklahoma 23–6, Texas A&M 27–17 and Michigan 41–27. The defense had 39 sacks, 82 tackles for loss and 23 takeaways. The offense gained 5,053 yards, but Texas also threw 16 interceptions, took 108 penalties for 906 yards and held the ball for 28 minutes per game.

Texas scored 35.8 points per game in 10 wins and 12.7 in three losses. The rebuild starts with that gap.— Josh Pate

The offseason changed the roster fast. Six Longhorns were drafted, including Anthony Hill Jr. at No. 60, Malik Muhammad at No. 124, Trey Moore at No. 130 and Michael Taaffe at No. 158. Twenty-seven players entered the portal, including four backs who produced 1,181 rushing yards and receivers Parker Livingstone and DeAndre Moore Jr., who combined for 1,048 yards and 10 touchdowns. Texas signed 22 transfers, adding Raleek Brown and Hollywood Smothers at running back; Cam Coleman and Sterling Berkhalter at receiver; and Melvin Siani, Laurence Seymore III, Dylan Sikorski and Paris Patterson on the offensive line.

Manning returns after 3,163 passing yards and 26 touchdowns, and Ryan Wingo returns after 834 receiving yards and seven scores. Trevor Goosby is back at left tackle. Colin Simmons returns after 12 sacks and 15.5 tackles for loss. New defensive coordinator Will Muschamp inherits Simmons, Jelani McDonald, Graceson Littleton and a deeper front, then adds Rasheem Biles, Bo Mascoe, Ian Geffrard and Zion Williams. Brown and Smothers need to give Texas a steadier run game behind a remodeled interior. Ohio State on September 12 is the first look.

No. 05 · TexasThe Offense

The championship case

Manning · The call sheet · New weapons · The interior bet

Arch Manning’s 3,163 passing yards, 26 touchdowns, seven interceptions and 61.4 percent completion rate set a solid baseline, but Texas averaged 35.8 points in 10 wins and 12.7 in three losses. Better rushing, steadier interior protection and a new skill group have to close that gap before the schedule accelerates.

Heading into the Sept. 5 opener against Texas State, Manning is the certainty in an offense returning six starters. He averaged 7.8 yards per attempt in his first full season as the starter and added 399 rushing yards with 10 touchdowns. When the run game stalled, Texas had too many obvious passing downs.

Photo slotArch ManningGame photo · Second-year starter, full command required
The résumé is real. Control is next. Manning accounted for 3,562 yards and 36 touchdowns passing and rushing in 2025. Texas needs his command to settle an overhauled supporting cast.

Manning has to handle more before the snap in his second season. Manning needs to identify leverage, set protection and punish rotations without waiting for a clean picture.

Texas rushed for 1,794 yards and averaged 138.0 per game. After the first fall scrimmage, Sarkisian said the staff had been “harping on physicality” and running the ball, then praised the first offensive-line unit for its protection and continuity. Texas can’t leave both safeties available for the pass on third down.

The backfield was rebuilt after Quintrevion Wisner, CJ Baxter Jr., Jerrick Gibson and Christian Clark transferred. Raleek Brown arrived after surpassing 1,000 yards at Arizona State while averaging 6.1 per carry, and Hollywood Smothers produced four 100-yard games at NC State. Freshman Derrek Cooper is pushing for work.

Cam Coleman gives Texas an X receiver, Ryan Wingo returns after 54 catches for 834 yards and seven touchdowns, and Emmett Mosley V brings 28 catches for 408 yards into the slot. Tight end is less settled after Jack Endries’ departure. Michael Masunas brought portal experience, while 6-foot-7, 260-pound Spencer Shannon has pushed the competition in camp and gives Manning a red-zone target.

One left guard claims the job, Baker stays inside, Siani settles at right tackle and Texas improves on 138.0 rushing yards per game while protecting Manning as it did in a 23-sack season.

If it doesn’t

The staff keeps changing combinations, interior games create pressure and Manning again carries third-and-long. The skill group spends too many snaps waiting on protection.

No. 05 · TexasThe Defense

Twenty-point-three is gone. What replaces the spine?

What left · What returns · What arrived · The communication clock

Texas returns 12 of the 39 sacks it produced in 2025 through Colin Simmons, but four drafted defenders leave a defense that allowed 20.3 points per game.

The 2025 defense allowed 20.3 points and 338.8 yards per game, held opponents to 64 conversions on 194 third downs and forced 23 turnovers. It gave up 29 at Florida and 35 at Georgia, the two regular-season games in which an opponent reached 29. Texas generated 39 sacks while allowing 23 and recorded seven interceptions. In three losses, Texas allowed 26.0 points per game while the offense scored 12.7. The defense had flaws, but it did not carry those losses alone.

Pate State original artThe movable pieceColin Simmons and the new geometry of the Texas front
Twelve sacks return. Four drafted defenders do not. Muschamp can align Simmons wide, reduce him inside or move him after the protection declares. The linebackers and secondary have to communicate behind him when he moves.

Anthony Hill Jr., Malik Muhammad, Trey Moore and Michael Taaffe combined for 204 tackles and 8.5 sacks before they were drafted. The portal also took Liona Lefau, Ty’Anthony Smith, Kobe Black, Warren Roberson, Derek Williams Jr., Elijah Barnes, Caleb Chester and Lavon Johnson. Simmons returns with 12 sacks, 15.5 tackles for loss and 15 quarterback hurries. Jelani McDonald led the team with 80 tackles, Graceson Littleton broke up six passes, and Hero Kanu, Alex January and Maraad Watson return interior snaps and size. Lance Jackson and Zina Umeozulu give Will Muschamp options opposite Simmons as he moves him around the front.

Texas added eight defenders from the portal, including Rasheem Biles, Bo Mascoe, Justin Cryer, Ian Geffrard and Zion Williams. Darius Snow, Markus Boswell and Nick Hudson add experience. Camp reporting has Mascoe emerging as a likely outside starter, and Steve Sarkisian has praised Cryer’s physicality and preparation. Geffrard holding the point at nose would free Biles and the linebackers while Simmons moves across the formation. Littleton can work in the slot, and McDonald has range, but the group must exchange calls correctly under tempo.

Cryer and Biles must replace the communication and production lost with Hill, Mascoe must handle a starting corner assignment, and McDonald must organize the back end while keeping his versatility. Sept. 12 against Ohio State brings motions, protection checks and third-and-medium. Nov. 14 at LSU is the tenth game. By then, the transition has to be settled.

Four blueprints · and their expiration dates

11. Crowd Manning’s interior clock

Double-A-gap pressure→Four-man games and simulated pressure

The tackles are the strength, so defenses can attack left guard, test Baker’s move inside and make Manning reset before Coleman and Wingo get downfield.

22. Put the new backs in protection

Light box and two-high shells→Late nickel pressure

Brown and Smothers arrived to repair the run game. Opponents can threaten pressure after the check and force the rebuilt backfield to find the extra rusher.

33. Make anyone but Simmons finish

Protection shaded toward No. 1→Chips, quick game and runs away from him

Simmons produced 12 of Texas’ 39 sacks last season. Slides and tight-end help put Jackson, Spence and Umeozulu in isolated rushes before the quarterback’s first answer opens.

44. Stress the new middle

Motion, bunch and condensed formations→Tempo into seams and option routes

Biles and Cryer are newcomers, Filsaime is being pushed, and Muschamp wants multiplicity. Offenses can hurry the communication, move Littleton and test the linebacker-safety exchange.

What breaks the blueprintBy Sept. 12, Texas needs a left guard, backs who handle pressure, a rush opposite Simmons and clean linebacker-safety communication. The next check comes at Tennessee on Sept. 26.

Five things that are actually true

No quarterback mythology, no portal accounting tricks

Manning returns; his offensive ecosystem does not.

Manning returns with 3,163 passing yards, 26 touchdowns and 85.4 percent of Texas' passing production. Texas returns 6 offensive starters but only 55.0 percent of its receiving production after Parker Livingstone and DeAndre Moore Jr. transferred and Jack Endries was drafted. Cam Coleman changes the talent level, but the receiver timing still has to be built.

The boldest bet is the offensive-line reshuffle.

Texas asks Wake Forest transfer Melvin Siani to play right tackle while Brandon Baker moves inside, Trevor Goosby anchors the left side and the guards remain unsettled. Ohio State on Sept. 12 brings the first look at Siani against SEC edge speed and the interior run surface.

The running back rebuild was a choice.

Four transfers accounted for 1,181 rushing yards in 2025: Quintrevion Wisner, CJ Baxter Jr., Jerrick Gibson and Christian Clark. Raleek Brown surpassed 1,000 yards at 6.1 per carry, and Hollywood Smothers had 4 100-yard games. Outside-zone potential has to hold up in SEC short yardage.

Simmons carries a large share of the transition.

Colin Simmons had 12 of Texas' 39 sacks and 15.5 tackles for loss last season. Six Longhorns were drafted, including Anthony Hill Jr., Malik Muhammad, Trey Moore and Michael Taaffe, so the linebacker and secondary communication still need answers.

The hidden opponent is self-inflicted yardage.

Texas took 108 penalties for 906 yards while averaging 28 minutes of possession per game. The defense forced 23 turnovers, but November leaves little room for pre-snap flags, lost field position and extended drives.

What goes wrong

I have Texas at 10-2, in the SEC title game and hosting as the No. 5 seed in the College Football Playoff. I have Texas beating Ohio State on Sept. 12 because Manning is in year 2 as the starter, the game is in Austin and Wingo, Coleman and the quarterback run element get a rebuilt defense early.

Texas has a 985.17 talent composite, 72.1 percent overall returning production and its returning quarterback. It has 55.0 percent of its receiving production back, a remade backfield and an offensive line changing multiple jobs.

Texas averaged about 138 rushing yards, stayed below 100 5 times and leaned on Manning in short yardage. Brown and Smothers bring proven explosiveness, but Siani at right tackle and Baker inside remain the personnel bet. Ohio State starts the grading; Tennessee on Sept. 26 brings the fuller answer.

Simmons returns with 12 sacks, but 4 of the 6 drafted Longhorns named here were Hill, Muhammad, Moore and Taaffe. Three November road games pressure a reconstructed secondary and its communication.

The floor is 8-4 and outside the playoff if the line reshuffle fails, the new backs can't create dependable early-down production and the secondary remains unsettled. The Championship Ceiling is a 12-0 regular season and a CFP semifinal run if the right side holds, Coleman makes bracket coverage expensive and the defense rushes 4 without exposing its rebuilt middle. I land at 10-2 because LSU and Texas A&M collect the late schedule tax. — JP
