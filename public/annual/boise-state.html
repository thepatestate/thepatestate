No. 04 · Boise StateThe Autopsy

Start with the autopsy

Why 2025 ended · What the offseason answered

As Boise State prepares to open at Oregon, the 2025 record reads 9–5 and a third straight Mountain West championship. The losses tell the other half: 7.6 points per defeat, exactly seven in four of five.

Boise State opened Aug. 28 with a 34–7 loss at South Florida, then scored seven in a 28–7 loss at Notre Dame on Oct. 4, a 30–7 home loss to Fresno State on Nov. 1 and a 17–7 loss at San Diego State on Nov. 16. Washington closed the season with a 38–10 LA Bowl win on Dec. 14. Boise State scored 38 points across five losses after scoring 38 in the Mountain West championship.

Photo slotDecember 14 · at WashingtonGame photo · the 38–10 LA Bowl loss
The bowl put the scoring split on the board one more time. Boise State reached its third straight Mountain West title, then scored 10 points in the bowl while Cutforth threw three second-half interceptions. The Broncos scored 38 points across five losses and 38 in the conference championship.

The offense averaged 42.2 points in nine wins and 7.6 in five losses. Boise State produced 2,512 rushing yards, 29 rushing touchdowns and 33 minutes of possession per game, but negative plays and obvious passing downs changed the offense. The Broncos allowed 26 sacks, committed 97 penalties for 832 yards and turned the ball over 21 times. Maddux Madsen threw nine interceptions; Max Cutforth threw five, including three in the second half against Washington.

The defense recorded 84 tackles for loss, 28 sacks and 14 interceptions while forcing 23 turnovers. It also allowed 2,273 rushing yards and 24.1 points per game; the five teams that beat Boise State averaged 29.4. Fresno State and Washington each reached 30 while Boise State scored 17 combined points.

In five losses, Boise State averaged 7.6 points and scored seven four times.— Josh Pate

Boise State left quarterback alone in the winter portal. Madsen returns as the starter, Cutforth is No. 2 and Zeke Martinez is behind them. Madsen completed 58.3 percent for 2,333 yards, 18 touchdowns and nine interceptions, missed the final three regular-season games with a lower-leg injury, then returned for the Mountain West championship and bowl. Spencer Danielson identified fewer sacks, penalties and giveaways after the season. Boise State opens Saturday, Sept. 5, at Oregon.

Kage Casey was Boise State’s only 2026 draft pick, while 19 players entered the portal, including leading tackler Ty Benefield and 574-yard receiver Chris Marshall. Eleven transfers arrived across the secondary, defensive line, offensive line, linebacker, running back and receiver. Boise State returns 66.3 percent of its production overall: 94.3 percent of passing production and 100.4 percent of rushing production, but 39.6 percent of receiving production.

No. 04 · Boise StateThe Offense

The championship case

Madsen · The boldest bet · Run gravity · The open line job

I put Boise State on the No. 4 seed line, while the Pate State Model keeps the Broncos outside its preseason top 25. The split is 94.3% of the passing production and 100.4% of the rushing production back against 39.6% of the receiving production; heading into the Oregon opener, Boise State needs a cleaner Madsen and a settled line.

Maddux Madsen completed 176 of 302 passes for 2,333 yards, 18 touchdowns and nine interceptions in 2025: 58.3% completions and 7.7 yards per attempt. He missed the final three regular-season games with a lower-leg injury, then returned for the Mountain West Championship and LA Bowl. Boise State averaged 42.2 points in nine wins and 7.6 in five losses, with seven points in four of those losses. It scored 56 against UNLV and disappeared in too many other games.

Photo slotMaddux MadsenGame photo · the third-year decision
The arm returns. The efficiency must change. Madsen has 94.3% returning passing production around him, but Boise State scored 38 offensive points across five losses. He needs fewer empty possessions.

Boise State left the winter portal without a quarterback, returning Madsen, Max Cutforth and Zeke Martinez. Cutforth won the No. 2 job after completing 95 of 167 passes for 1,059 yards, four touchdowns and five interceptions; three came in the second half of the 38-10 LA Bowl loss to Washington. Madsen needs to improve on 58.3%, and the offense needs fewer than the 26 sacks, 21 turnovers and 97 penalties that created bad downs.

The supplied reporting does not identify the coordinator or 2026 play-caller. Danielson named turnovers, penalties and sacks as the three offensive numbers that must change. Madsen matters, but protection, procedure and down-and-distance belong to the whole offense.

Dylan Riley ran 195 times for 1,125 yards and 10 touchdowns at 5.8 per carry. Sire Gaines added 812 yards, eight touchdowns and 5.0 per carry. Boise State ran for 2,512 yards and 29 touchdowns, and 100.4% of its rushing production returns. Riley is also the primary kickoff returner. The backs can keep second-level defenders downhill and keep Madsen out of obvious passing downs.

Only 39.6% of receiving production returns, and Chris Marshall transferred after leading the 2025 group with 574 yards at 19.1 per catch. The returning order at receiver and tight end is not identified in the supplied reporting. Up front, fourth-round pick Kage Casey is gone, Jason Steele and Stewart Taufa alternated in Saturday’s scrimmage, and Daylon Metoyer and Jacob Tracy remained in another competition. That is a lot of communication to settle before Oregon.

No. 04 · Boise StateThe Defense

The defense has havoc. It needs a floor.

What left · what returns · what arrived · six days to Oregon

Boise State produced 84 tackles for loss, 28 sacks and 14 interceptions last season while allowing 24.1 points and 162.4 rushing yards per game. heading into the Oregon opener, the first Pac-12 defense has to replace production in the middle and settle a secondary remade by the portal.

Boise State allowed 337 points in 14 games, held opponents to 62 conversions on 184 third downs, forced 23 turnovers and gave up 175.5 passing yards per game. Across five losses, the Broncos allowed 29.4 points per game: 34 at South Florida, 30 against Fresno State and 38 against Washington. The offense scored 10 points or fewer in every loss, but the run defense gave up 2,273 yards in 14 games.

Pate State original artBlue under pressureA disruptive defense enters a new league with its interior rotation still under construction
The havoc survived. The weekly floor has to rise. Boise State returns a 6-sack, 13-TFL pressure source in Jayden Virgin-Morgan, but replaces its leading tackler and opens at Oregon with competition still active through the middle of the defense.

Ty Benefield transferred to LSU after leading Boise State with 105 tackles, 8.5 tackles for loss and two interceptions. Jaylen Webb, Ja’Bree Bickham and Davon Banks also left through the portal. No Boise State defender was selected in the 2026 NFL Draft; offensive tackle Kage Casey was the program’s lone pick. Benefield’s tackles, alignment flexibility and production now have to come from more than one player.

Jayden Virgin-Morgan returns with 6 sacks, 13 tackles for loss and 12 quarterback hurries. Jaden Mickey had 7 tackles for loss and five pass breakups, and Boen Phelps made 66 tackles. Jake Ripp had 43 tackles in 2025 but missed camp time with a hamstring and was expected back during Oregon preparation. Spencer Danielson said Logan Brantley had returned and was doing good work, while the middle-linebacker rotation remained unresolved entering game week.

The portal brought Mikaio Edward from Central Washington to the line, Brantley from Kansas at linebacker, Cam Jamerson and JeRico Washington Jr. at corner, and Taebron Bennie-Powell and Roman Tillmon at safety. The official two-deep is due Monday, one day after this page closes. Danielson named David Latu and Edward as the leading interior starters, but said the next three or four rotational jobs lacked consistency. Oregon gets a new interior and an unfinished rotation. Latu and Edward need to hold early downs, and Virgin-Morgan needs pass-rush downs.

No. 04 · Boise StateThe Film Room

How opponents will attack Boise State

Four blueprints · and the dates when they can expire

11. Make the new interior defend downhill

Spread formations and horizontal motion→Duo, counter and split-flow runs

Boise State allowed 2,273 rushing yards last season. Latu and Edward entered game week as the leading interior pair without a settled five- or six-man rotation behind them. Oregon can widen the box and run at those new combinations.

22. Change the protection picture after the snap

Five- and six-man pressure surfaces→Simulated pressure with tackle-end games

The offense allowed 26 sacks and threw 14 interceptions in 2025. Opponents can crowd the line, drop an apparent rusher and twist through either unresolved offensive-line job.

33. Make the rebuilt receiver room win outside

An extra defender near Riley and Gaines→Press-man with a low-hole robber

Riley and Gaines combined for 1,937 rushing yards, but Boise State returns 39.6% of its receiving production and lost Marshall's 19.1 yards per catch. Defenses can crowd the run and challenge Ford, Bates and the unsettled third receiver outside.

44. Stress the new safety communication

Bunch, motion and condensed splits→Tempo, switches and vertical crossers

Benefield's 105 tackles left with him, and the projected safety replacements arrived through the portal. Opponents can use traffic before the snap, then tempo and crossers against a reassembled secondary.

What breaks the blueprintRiley and Gaines can punish light boxes at 5.8 and 5.0 yards per carry. Madsen has to identify the post-snap rusher and avoid negative downs. Ford and Bates have to win isolated coverage. The new secondary has to handle motion without giving up vertical leverage. Oregon on Sept. 5 comes first; Memphis on Sept. 12 gives Boise State a second look.

What would change the grade

Eight falsifiable inputs · no quiet revisions

Move me up

  • Madsen completes at least 65% of his passes with no more than one turnover-worthy result at Oregon on Sept. 5.
  • The offensive line allows no more than one sack at Oregon and starts the same five against Memphis on Sept. 12.
  • Ford and Bates combine for at least 150 receiving yards in one of the first two games, showing the 39.6% returning-production figure understates the room.
  • Boise State holds either Oregon or Memphis below 4.5 yards per carry, with Latu and Edward both in the primary interior rotation.

Move me down

  • Madsen commits two or more turnovers at Oregon, carrying the 2025 ball-security problem into the new season.
  • Boise State allows at least three sacks in either of its first two games, especially through one of the two open offensive-line jobs.
  • No receiver reaches 75 yards against Oregon or Memphis, letting defenses overload the Riley-Gaines run game.
  • The secondary allows three or more passing touchdowns of at least 20 yards through Sept. 12 on coverage or communication errors.

Five things that are actually true

No sales pitch; the disagreement stays

My No. 4 seed and the Model's top 25 don't match.

My Aug. 19 bracket gives Boise State the No. 4 seed. The Model has the Broncos outside its preseason top 25. Oregon on Sept. 5 and Memphis on Sept. 12 bring that gap forward fast.

Three quarterbacks make standing pat a personnel bet.

Boise State returns Maddux Madsen, Max Cutforth and Zeke Martinez after adding no winter-portal quarterback. Madsen completed 58.3% for 2,333 yards, 18 touchdowns and 9 interceptions. Cutforth threw 5 interceptions, including 3 in the second half of the 38–10 bowl loss to Washington. Madsen has to cut negative plays, and Cutforth has to be steady if needed.

The 100.4% rushing share carries the roster.

Boise State returns a 100.4% PPA share of its rushing production. Dylan Riley ran for 1,125 yards and 10 touchdowns at 5.8 per carry; Sire Gaines added 812 yards and 8 scores at 5.0. But 2,512 team rushing yards came with 4 games of 7 offensive points and another with 10.

The defense had 84 tackles for loss and allowed 2,273 rushing yards.

The Broncos had 84 tackles for loss, 28 sacks and 14 interceptions. Jayden Virgin-Morgan had 13 tackles for loss and 6 sacks. They allowed 2,273 rushing yards, or 162.4 per game.

The 7-game Pac-12 slate offers recovery later.

Boise State has 7 Pac-12 games and 6 home dates, but Oregon and Memphis arrive before Labor Day is 2 weeks old. An 0–2 start leaves little path to the No. 4 seed I published.

What goes wrong

I have Boise State losing at Oregon and winning its other 10 regular-season games. That puts the Broncos at 10–1, atop the Pac-12 and in the playoff as the No. 4 seed. The Model has Boise State outside the top 25, and I own that difference.

The case starts with 100.4% of the rushing-production share returning, Madsen's 2 full seasons as a starter, and a defense with 84 tackles for loss and 14 interceptions. Boise State doesn't need to beat Oregon to control the conference, but it needs a functional offense and enough health for Memphis 7 days later.

The passing game is the problem. Madsen completed 58.3% with 9 interceptions, the offense allowed 26 sacks, and 39.6% of receiving production returns. Boise State scored 7 points in 4 losses and 10 in the fifth. The Championship Ceiling needs fewer empty possessions.

The 10–1 call breaks if the quarterback room keeps giving the ball away, the new receivers don't create explosives, or the defense keeps allowing 162.4 rushing yards per game. Sept. 5 tests protection, Sept. 12 tests the response, and Oct. 24 tests the road formula.

My floor is 8–3, with losses at Oregon and in 2 of the Memphis, Fresno State, Washington State, Oregon State and San Diego State group, followed by a missed playoff. My Championship Ceiling is 11–0, a Pac-12 championship and a playoff win before the run ends. The pick is 10–1, Pac-12 champion and No. 4 seed, not national champion. — JP
