# Article conventions from Josh's 2026-09-01 package — review and resulting rules

Source: "Article visuals for THE PATE STATE" (Josh, 5 HTML attachments: visual system
v2.2 + wire/column/annual mockups) and his 5:38 PM follow-up (sidebar redesign — a
frontend conversation, scheduled separately, not covered here). This doc records how
the mockups' WRITING conventions were reconciled with the standing desk rules.

## Where Josh's mockups overrode standing rules
1. **Labeled sections are IN for story/analysis depth.** The wire mockup is built as a
   numbered arc: What Happened → Why This One Matters → What Most People Are Missing →
   Next Man Up → What the Coaches Can Actually Change → The Pate State Read → What to
   Watch Next. The old blanket ban on a "why it matters" section now applies only to
   items/briefs (where stakes stay woven in prose). AI-speak inside prose ("this
   matters because") stays banned everywhere.
2. **The breaking outlet is named once, in the lead attribution** ("On3 reported
   Thursday"). The ban on outlet-name stuffing elsewhere in prose stands.
3. **Event-anchored counts are durable and allowed** ("Sept. 5, sixteen days from the
   announcement"). The ban on writing-moment time ("five days before the opener",
   "today", "this week") stands — Josh's own annual has zero of those.

## Rules adopted from the mockups
- Why This One Matters ARGUES with numbers; assertion is not stakes.
- What Most People Are Missing = the story under the story: one non-obvious mechanism
  (the Pastore mockup's "certainty loss", the Whitmore mockup's board pivot).
- News-chain checklist for injury/portal/coaching/commitment stories: what happened →
  who's next → biggest effect → game most affected (dated) → the desk's read.
- Watch List items must be checkable from the couch or the box score, each with its tell.
- Return timelines are a range, never a date, unless the program announced one.
- Coaching-adjustment framing: levers as taxes and trade-offs, never doom.
- Josh columns (Miami/ACC exemplar): open with the claim + the number that carries it;
  direct address invited ("So name the alternative."); one LINE WORTH KEEPING with its
  grading date; the honest concession carries dates ("due in September"); close by
  planting the flag — the pick, the one result that reopens it, when it grades.
- Never ghost a Josh position: if he hasn't taken it, it doesn't render (already law
  via the on-record ledger; reaffirmed by the visual system's Pate Take rule).

## Explicitly NOT done here (frontend, for the sidebar chat)
The Core 20 / Franchise / Advanced visual components, their data contracts, the wire
page layout (kicker chips, numbers rail, facts rail, zviz bars), and the sidebar
redesign. Those are build work against the mockups, not prompt work.
