Twenty visuals that answer the questions fans actually ask

v2.0 is a simplification, not an expansion. The core library is twenty visuals, each tied to a question a normal college football fan naturally has — who wins, why, who matters, what changed, what to watch. Everything technical lives on a gated Advanced shelf and only appears when an article's central argument requires it. The companion spec (PATE-STATE-VISUAL-SYSTEM-v2.md, v2.1) replaces prior versions wholesale. v2.1 adds the ten Franchises, restores the full One-on-One and the Gauntlet name, and trims the Film Room shelf.

C-01 · NEW

The Pate Take

The signature. Three slots, never more: My read · What most people are missing · The Hinge — the one thing the whole opinion rests on, with the consequence stated both directions. The footer carries the running record of graded Takes instead of a self-assessed confidence score: accountability, not declaration.

Answers: "What does Josh actually think?"
Fits: nearly everything. One per article, maximum. Never ghost-written — if Josh hasn't taken the position, the box doesn't render. Every row is gradeable: the read, the miss, the hinge, the record.

THE PATE TAKE
My read
Georgia is the better team, but Alabama has the one matchup that can make the talent gap irrelevant.
What most people are missing
Georgia's secondary isn't the concern. Alabama's ability to keep Beck off schedule is.
The Hinge
Georgia's pass protection. If it holds, Georgia wins. If it doesn't, this becomes a coin flip.
— JPTakes this season: 14–5 · This one grades Nov. 14
The tab, the border, and the — JP sign-off never change. This box is the brand's most-hunted pixel real estate; nothing else on the site gets the red tab.
