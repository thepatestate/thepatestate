Connecting the YouTube Channel

One invite, about two minutes, no passwords shared. This lets the site pull the show’s official captions and video data directly from the channel instead of working around it.

Why this helps

Accurate quotesEvery article quotes Josh verbatim, checked word-for-word against the episode transcript. Official captions make that bulletproof.
No workaroundsToday the site reads public data and scrapes captions through a third party. Channel access removes the fragile middle-man.
Nothing changesThe channel, uploads, and comments stay exactly as they are. This is read access for the website’s pipeline — that’s it.

What to do

Open YouTube Studio — go to studio.youtube.com signed in to the account that runs @JoshPateCFB.
Open permissions — in the left sidebar click Settings (gear icon, bottom), then Permissions. If Google shows you a “Brand Account permissions” page instead, that’s the same thing — keep going.
Invite this address — click Invite and enter:
the.pate.state@gmail.com
This is the website’s own dedicated account — it exists only to run thepatestate.com.
Choose the role “Manager”, then click Save / Done. Manager is the role that lets the site’s pipeline read captions and video data through YouTube’s official tools. It cannot delete the channel.
Tell us it’s done — reply to whoever sent you this. We accept the invite and handle everything else. You never need to touch it again.

The ground rules

What we will never do with this access: upload, edit, or delete videos; change titles, thumbnails, or settings; touch comments; or post anything. It powers the website’s reading pipeline only.
  • Revocable anytime — same screen, remove the address, done.
  • No passwords involved — never share the channel password with us or anyone.
  • No rush — the site runs fine today; this just makes it sturdier before the season.
