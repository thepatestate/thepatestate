1 · What You're Receiving

thepatestate.com is a self-running college football site built around Josh's show. It was designed to the specification in the Operations Manual v1.2 (in the repo at docs/) with one governing rule: the site publishes autonomously, except anything in Josh's voice or under Josh's byline — that always waits for a human click.

  • The Show — every episode, pulled automatically from YouTube minutes after it publishes.
  • The Notebook — AI-drafted companion articles for every episode, grounded in the real transcript with Josh's verbatim quotes deep-linked to the video, waiting in an approval queue.
  • The Wire — an autonomous breaking-news desk that monitors ESPN/CBS/Yahoo feeds every 10 minutes, writes attributed news items, and auto-publishes verified full stories with Josh's real archived takes attached.
  • The Playbook — a daily 6:00 AM ET email to registered citizens, assembled from real site content.
  • Citizenship — free registration (magic link or Google) storing fans in your own database.
  • Scores & Schedules — real FBS schedule data live now; scores flow automatically on gamedays.
  • JP Poll, Pick'em, Playoffs, Recruiting, Tailgate, Store — designed and built; the interactive engines (voting, picks, brackets) are labeled preseason previews and activate as those features launch with the season.

The one daily human job

Open thepatestate.sanity.studio → Approval Queue. New AI drafts sit in state ai-drafted. Read the draft (5–10 minutes — the quotes are verbatim from the transcript and deep-linked, so spot-checking is fast). Fix anything you want, then change workflowState → approved. The article publishes to the live site within a minute, with its hero image, video embeds, and SEO fields already in place.

Drafts flagged ⚠ lowConfidence deserve a closer read — it means the transcript was missing or a quote couldn't be verified word-for-word.

Editorial rules already enforced in software

  • Bylines: "Josh Pate" appears only on content Josh wrote or approved. AI staff coverage is bylined "The Pate State Staff" (third-person). Autonomous news is "The Wire Desk" with an AI disclosure on every story.
  • Quotes: anything inside quotation marks is machine-verified as a word-for-word transcript match. The system physically cannot publish an invented Josh quote.
  • Wire sourcing: every story names its source in sentence one, passes an independent AI fact-check against the sources, and blocks a banned-inference list (no motive speculation, no health prognosis, no "sources tell The Pate State"). Injury/legal stories use a sober register with zero humor.
  • Corrections: never edit facts silently — strike through and append a timestamped correction.
  • The full standards live in the repo: prompts/voice-manual.md and prompts/wire-desk-manual.md (Josh's own documents, implemented as written).

Runbook — the five most likely situations

SituationWhat to do
A wire story is wrongOpen Studio → Wire Story → edit with a strikethrough + timestamped correction line, or unpublish (delete) to retract. The story's sources are listed on every item.
Pause ALL wire publishingSupabase dashboard → Database → Extensions → pg_cron → unschedule wire-monitor (or ask a developer to run select cron.unschedule('wire-monitor')).
An article draft is badJust leave it un-approved (nothing publishes without the click), or delete it in Studio.
Stop the daily emailUnschedule send-playbook the same way as the wire, or remove RESEND_API_KEY from Vercel env.
Site looks broken after a code changeVercel dashboard → Deployments → promote the previous deployment (instant rollback).

6 · Transfer Checklist

Do these in order, on a call together if possible. The Gmail root makes most of it simple.

  • Hand over the.pate.state@gmail.com (password + set YOUR phone as 2FA and recovery). This is the master key — after this step, your team controls recovery for everything.
  • Change the Gmail password and add 2FA immediately.
  • Receive the password-manager share with all service passwords + API keys (never email these).
  • GitHub: accept owner invite to the thepatestate org; remove Isaac when comfortable.
  • Vercel: accept team ownership transfer; verify env variables are intact (Settings → Environment Variables).
  • Cloudflare: confirm domain + DNS access; update billing card for the annual renewal.
  • Sanity: accept admin invite to org orA50NKiO; add Josh/producer as Studio editors (they only ever need the Studio, not the code). Note: a second, empty Sanity account with a typo name ("thepatestatee") exists — delete it.
  • Supabase, Anthropic, Resend, BFL, Supadata, CFBD: log in via the Gmail root or transferred passwords; update billing cards where applicable (Anthropic and BFL hold small credit balances).
  • Rotate the Supabase database password and the Sanity write token after transfer (both were used during the build; rotating voids any residual exposure). A developer does this in ~15 minutes: change in Supabase/Sanity dashboards → update .env.local + Vercel env → redeploy.
  • Update PLAYBOOK_POSTAL_ADDRESS in Vercel env to your preferred business mailing address (CAN-SPAM footer).
  • Decide who holds the daily approval seat (the §22 "human monitor") and bookmark the Studio on their machine.
