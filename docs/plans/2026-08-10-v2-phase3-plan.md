Source: `docs/pate-state-v2-brief.md` §4 (spec), §12 Phase 3 (scope +
acceptance). One universal template at /teams/[slug]; launch the ~24 most
active fanbases as deep hubs; every other FBS program gets a directory
entry until its hub is ready. Acceptance: hubs feel like complete
destinations (not filtered article lists); boards integrated; articles
auto-populate by team; incomplete pages noindex; every visible number shows
its source or update time.

rosters (125 players), 2025 final records, 2026 portal (4.4k entries),
articles+wire by team from Sanity, Josh's verbatim quote archive by team
from Supabase, team porches from Phase 2, team_follows from Phase 1.

never invented numbers (§0.1).
