How to play

The basics
1

Pick the teams you think are the last four standing. Your 1,000 points split across them. 4 teams = 250 each. 10 teams = 100 each.

2

Crown your Final Four and call the title-game score. Week 1 pays full points; each later week pays 10% less. Doors close after Week 4.

3

Your weekly score follows the JP Poll, then the playoff rankings. At the finish, only your teams in the real Final Four score.

The rules

Build a bubble of 1–20 teams. Your 1,000 points split equally across them. Ranked teams earn a share of those points each week; unranked teams earn 1%.

Lock by Week 4. Week 1 pays 100%, Week 2 pays 90%, Week 3 pays 80%, and Week 4 pays 70%. Crown up to four teams from your bubble, picking your champion first, and predict the championship score.

After locking, you get one swap before the playoff. It costs 20 points plus 5 for each week after Week 1. The fee stays deducted. Your original crowned picks and score call stay fixed, even if you swap a crowned team.

At the finish, each real Final Four team in your bubble earns its full share; all other teams earn zero. Tied scores go to the correct champion pick, then the most crowned Final Four hits, then the smallest total error across the winner’s and loser’s predicted points. Remaining ties share the win. Time never breaks a tie.
