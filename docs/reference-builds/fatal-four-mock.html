How Fatal Four works

Plain fan language

The share

You get 1,000 points to spread across a bubble of 1 to 20 FBS teams. Each team's share is 1,000 ÷ your team count, and four is the floor — one team, or four, each share is 250. Ten teams: 100 apiece. Twenty: 50.

Late entry docks you

Lock in before the season and every share is worth 100%. Week 2 pays 90%, Week 3 80%, Week 4 70%. After Week 4 the window shuts and you're out until next year.

One swap. That's it.

One team out, one team in, once a season. The new team inherits the old share. The fee is 20 points times the current week — 20 in Week 1, 200 in Week 10 — and it comes straight off your score. No swaps after Week 10.

Poll slice

Your live score is what you'd bank if the season ended today: each share times its team's slice of the poll.

Season end

Each team takes the better of two numbers: what the playoff paid it — 20% for making the 12-team field, 50% for reaching the quarterfinal, 100% for the Final Four — or its slice of the final poll. Ties break to the smaller bubble, then to whoever entered earliest.
