### The approval lane: everything that carries Josh's byline or opinion. Where the porch runs at 100%.

**Load with:** `01-constitution.md` + `02-voice-bible.md` + `07-current-state.md` + the consistency ledger + the Josh file for the subject. Covers: GD, RK, PR, MD (Josh side), PS derivatives, PE, FA, CC Josh-voice pieces, NR-02, and show-derived columns. Reference build for register: `annual-ohio-state-v6-print.html`.

## 1. BYLINES & APPROVAL

- **"Josh Pate" + "— JP"** publish ONLY after his (or his editor's) approval click. Never auto-applied.
- **Approval status is internal, never reader-facing (v1.1).** No page chip, kicker, or label ever says "Approved Byline" or exposes workflow language. Reader-facing chrome carries product language instead: "Josh's Read · Logged to the Ledger." The accountability signal is the Ledger, not the CMS.
- Un-approved analysis in the same register: byline **"The Pate State Staff"**, first-person singular dialed to zero, house arguments labeled as the house's.
- Model-voice pieces (GD-11, MD-01/02/04/05): machine register, staff byline, inputs always shown.
- Show-derived columns: Josh byline after approval, with 1–3 verbatim spoken blockquotes and inline timestamped embeds (▶ EMBED · m:ss) per Voice Bible §10.

## 2. STANDARD FEATURE ARCHITECTURE (🟢, 800–1,500 words)

- **Cold open (porch):** disclaimer/observation → commit anyway. 2–4 short paragraphs.
- **The claim, stated plainly, inside the first 150 words.** This isn't a mystery novel.
- **The case:** 2–4 sections. Each blends porch framing → Film Room mechanism → honest counterpoint where the facts create one (never mechanically) → kicker line that passes the Clarity Law. Vary the joints — no scaffold phrase recurs as a fixed beat (anti-template rule).
- **The "quickly" section:** sweep secondary items in one confident paragraph.
- **The flag plant:** the final answer, unhedged, one mechanism sentence as the reason.
- **The porch close:** receipts, the invitation to disagree, one CTA, the test close with its date, sign-off per byline rules.

## 3. DEFINITIVE ARCHITECTURE (🟣, 1,500–2,500+, no ceiling)

Numbered PART I–VI/VII structure: Thesis → deep cases (2–3 parts) → Awards → schedule/context table → "The Things Nobody Puts in the Preview" (3–4 items) → flag plant → porch close. Punctuated by the callout-box system (§4). Annual-tier team pieces route to `05-spec-annual.md` instead.

## 4. THE CALLOUT-BOX SYSTEM (definitive-class furniture)

Blockquote formatting with these exact headers:
- **📋 THE STATE OF THE STATE** — the whole situation in 6–8 scannable lines, always including the JP Poll number, the Model's position, and any Josh-vs-Predictor gap. Directly after the cold open.
- **🔢 3 NUMBERS THAT MATTER** — three stats, one sentence of why each. Load-bearing numbers only — and every caption passes the Clarity Law (no "the season lives inside one arrow"; yes "the entire season hinges on this stat").
- **💪 STRENGTHS / ⚠️ WEAKNESSES** — four items each, every item a mechanism.
- **🏆 THE AWARDS BOARD** — five named awards minimum (🔗 Linchpin of the Season — can be a person, a unit, a date, or a medical file · 📈 Breakout · 🎯 Most Important Newcomer/Returnee · 🚀 Most Improved · ⚔️ Toughest Stretch). Each award is an argument.
- **Schedule tier table** — real dates, the fixed tier set (🟢 Expected Win · 🟡 Handle Business · 🟠 Upset Watch · 🔴 Season-Definer), one honest sentence per game; the toughest game, THE trap, and the deciding stretch named beneath.
- **🗳️ CITIZEN PULSE** — 2–3 one-tap questions with splits and one dry aside.
- **[PULLQUOTE]** — one marked line per article (only if the piece naturally produced one — never manufactured for the slot). **[EMBED]** — inline at the exact timestamp of the argument for show-derived pieces; end-of-article embed is the floor otherwise.

## 6. GD-08 — THE PATE STATE GAME GRADE (template)

Position-by-position letter grades, both teams, one table: QB · RB · WR/TE · OL · DL · LB · Secondary · Coaching · Depth · Special Teams, plus the Model row (X% / Y%). Closing line, always: **PATE EDGE: [TEAM]** — one mechanism sentence, no hedge. Every grade below B- gets one justifying clause: grades are claims; claims get mechanisms. Rendered as a shareable card; graded against the result in Sunday's GD-16, with a standing "What We Got Wrong" note when the edge missed. Grades criticize units, never dunk on kids.

## 7. RANKINGS MECHANICS (RK types)

Movement requires stated inputs — a ranking without inputs is astrology. Per placement: the mechanism, the AP/Coaches delta where applicable, and the Model's counter-position. Market-gap lists (RK-10) use "the market is too high/low on" framing exclusively, run as paired articles, and log to the Ledger with grading dates. Coach/pressure lists (RK-07, PE-03) are trajectory and structure — buyout math in plain English, analysis never glee. Every list is a team-page and player-page building operation.

## 8. PREDICTIONS & THE LEDGER (PR types + everything with a pick)

Every prediction: timestamped, consistency-ledger-checked before publish, written to the Ledger at publish with a named grading date. Cross-referenced picks name their siblings ("consistent with what I planted in the Texas annual"). Breaking an old pick happens loudly — a receipts moment, never a silent edit. PR-06 The Receipts grades Josh's misses first and credits citizens' best calls by name. The Ledger product surfaces: Josh / Model / Citizens season scoreboards, the open-claims list (⏳ OPEN / ✅ HIT / ❌ MISS), and claim pages (date, original argument, original video, what happened, grade, what we learned).

## 9. MD-03 — JOSH vs. THE MODEL

The weekly disagreement docket plus season standings. Stage the gap honestly: the Model's number with inputs, Josh's override with his mechanism, neither side softened. "Here's why I'm overriding the machine" IS the content. Every disagreement is scheduled for grading.
